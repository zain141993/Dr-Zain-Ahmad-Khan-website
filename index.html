About

A clinician's eye, a teacher's patience

My work spans three hospitals in Karachi, bringing together clinical care, research, and teaching. Most days start in clinic, seeing patients directly — but the job doesn't stop there. I also dig into the data behind respiratory care, with a particular focus on South Asia, where disease patterns and available resources often look different from what's described in standard textbooks.

Research is a steady part of my practice too, aimed at making everyday respiratory decisions more evidence-based rather than guesswork. And a meaningful share of my time goes into teaching — working with medical students and residents to build the next generation of respiratory care.

Clinical Focus

Where I spend most of my clinical time

01

Community-Acquired Pneumonia

Severity scoring, biomarker-augmented decision-making, and evidence-based management tailored to local presentation patterns.

02

Asthma & COPD

Long-term airway disease management, phenotyping, and helping patients understand and control their condition day to day.

03

Chest Imaging

CT chest interpretation for complex, infective, and mediastinal disease — and teaching trainees how to read them.

04

Lung Cancer

Evaluation of suspicious pulmonary lesions, diagnostic pathways, staging work-up, and coordinated respiratory care.

05

Tuberculosis

Assessment and management of pulmonary TB, including persistent symptoms, abnormal imaging, and treatment-related respiratory problems.

06

Pulmonary Embolism

Diagnosis and longitudinal care for pulmonary embolism and venous thromboembolism, with attention to risk and follow-up.

07

Pleural Disease

Evaluation of pleural effusions, pneumothorax, and other pleural conditions using imaging and evidence-based management.

08

Interstitial Lung Disease

Approach to unexplained breathlessness, diffuse lung disease, fibrosis, and multidisciplinary diagnostic evaluation.

09

Sleep & Breathing Disorders

Assessment of sleep-related breathing problems, including snoring, suspected sleep apnoea, and related daytime symptoms.
